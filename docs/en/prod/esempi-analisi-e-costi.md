# Work analysis and cost examples

[Italiano](../../prod/esempi-analisi-e-costi.md) | **English**

The following dataset is entirely synthetic. It helps explain and verify the five **Analysis** views; it does not describe data present in the installation and was not entered while writing. Amounts are in EUR, and totals were checked with integer calculations, applying the server's per-entry rounding.

## 1. Starting data

Use a lab with no other entries on the selected dates, or filters isolating this dataset. The example period is **12–13 October 2026**, inclusive. The product allows future dates, but real use must follow organizational time-tracking rules.

### People

| Person | Profile | Example condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marta | Manager | Logs personal hours and views aggregates |
| Elena | Operator | Contributor on projects A and B |
| Lucia | Operator | Contributor on projects C and E |
| Davide | Operator | Contributor on project D |

Grants cover both the test's current date and work dates. Each person enters their own rows from their own session; Marta cannot directly assign hours to colleagues from the personal form.

### Contracts and projects

Register a fictional active supplier and three contracts. Use the `ANA-` prefix to distinguish codes from other guides' cases.

| Contract | EUR value | Meaning |

| ANA-CND, Accordo da valorizzare | Empty | Unknown value, distinct from zero |
| ANA-C00, Accordo senza corrispettivo | 0.00 | Explicit zero |

| Project | Current contract | Example name |
| --- | --- | --- |

A and B share the same contract: this allows checking that its value is not counted twice. Leave ANA-CND's value field empty, rather than entering `0`.

## 2. By period view

As Manager, select **Analysis → By period**, From `2026-10-12`, To `2026-10-13`, with no other filters, and press **Apply**.

| Day | Hours | Minutes | Entries | Distinct projects | Distinct users |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 12 October | 5 h 15 min | 315 | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| 13 October | 3 h 45 min | 225 | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| **Period** | **9 h 00 min** | **540** | **10** | **5** | **4** |

Distinct counts are not summed across days: four users appear on both, so the period contains four people, not eight. There are five projects in the period, even though each day involves four.

Pressing the 12 October day switches to projects for that date alone. The summary becomes 315 minutes and five entries; project E has no hours that day and does not generate an invented empty row.

| A | 4 h 00 min | 240 | 3 | 2 |
| B | 2 h 30 min | 150 | 3 | 2 |

## 4. By user view

Without project or user filters, choose **By user**.

| Person | Hours | Minutes | Entries | Distinct projects |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Elena | 5 h 00 min | 300 | 4 | 2 |
| Marta | 1 h 30 min | 90 | 2 | 2 |
| Lucia | 1 h 30 min | 90 | 2 | 2 |
| Davide | 1 h 00 min | 60 | 2 | 1 |
| **Total** | **9 h 00 min** | **540** | **10** | **5 distinct projects** |

If Elena opens her analyses, her summary is always limited to 300 minutes, four entries and two projects, including in By user. She will see neither Marta's, Lucia's or Davide's hours nor a filter for choosing a colleague.

If Davide is deactivated in the lab after loading the dataset, his 60 minutes remain in the Manager's reports and he is marked as deactivated. Simply blocking the account does not delete them. Revoking Elena's project access instead preserves her previous personal analyses, but prevents new hours without another grant.

## 5. Contract value view

This view is available to the Manager. For the full period and no additional filters, the expected result is:

| Contract | Projects with hours | Linked hours | Minutes | Displayed value |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| ANA-C10 | 2, A and B | 6 h 30 min | 390 | 10,000.00 EUR |
| ANA-CND | 1, C | 1 h 00 min | 60 | Undefined |
| ANA-C00 | 1, E | 0 h 30 min | 30 | 0.00 EUR |

The summary must distinguish:

- **Three distinct contracts** linked to filtered work.
- **Total known value: 10,000.00 EUR**, counted only once for ANA-C10.
- **One contract without a specified value**, ANA-CND.
- **60 minutes without a contract**, from project D.
- The work summary remains **540 minutes and 10 entries**: it includes D too.

ANA-C10's value is not multiplied by two because it serves two projects. Nor is it divided in proportion to hours. Selecting only Elena, project B and 13 October leaves 30 minutes of work, but the displayed contract value remains **the full 10,000.00 EUR**.

This view does not calculate turnover, margin, accrued revenue or the amount to collect. Filter dates select hours, not contract installments. A contract without work in the period does not appear simply because it is active or its dates match the filter.

## 6. Labor cost view

Choose **Cost of hours** with the full period. Rows group person–project pairs, first pricing each entry with the rate valid on its date.

| Person | Project | Minutes | Entries | Minutes with rate | Minutes without rate | Estimated cost |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Elena | A | 210 | 2 | 210 | 0 | 219.00 EUR |
| Elena | B | 90 | 2 | 90 | 0 | 93.00 EUR |
| Marta | A | 30 | 1 | 30 | 0 | 45.00 EUR |
| Marta | B | 60 | 1 | 60 | 0 | 90.00 EUR |
| Lucia | C | 60 | 1 | 60 | 0 | 0.00 EUR |
| Lucia | E | 30 | 1 | 30 | 0 | 0.00 EUR |
| Davide | D | 60 | 2 | 0 | 60 | Unavailable |
| **Total** | **5 projects** | **540** | **10** | **480** | **60** | **447.00 EUR, partial estimate** |

Example calculation for Elena on A: 120 minutes on 12 October at 60 EUR/hour are worth 120 EUR; 90 minutes on 13 October at 66 EUR/hour are worth 99 EUR. The aggregate row is worth 219 EUR. The current 66 EUR rate is not applied to all five of that person's hours.

Known cost totals by day are 225 EUR on 12 October and 222 EUR on 13 October. By person, they are Elena 312 EUR, Marta 135 EUR and Lucia zero. Davide's cost remains unknown: **447 EUR is not the full cost of the nine hours**.

Lucia's zero rate and contract ANA-C00's zero value are two independent facts. Lucia has known zero cost even on C, whose contract has no value. Hourly cost is not inferred from contract value.

## 7. Rounding: a separate case

This case is not part of the previous ten rows. Prepare it on a separate project and interval to avoid changing reference totals.

At **1 EUR/hour**, a one-minute entry is worth 100/60 cents, rounded to **2 cents**. Rounding occurs for each row before summing:

| Entries | Total minutes | Resulting cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Three 1-minute rows | 3 | 0.02 + 0.02 + 0.02 = **0.06 EUR** |
| One 3-minute row | 3 | **0.05 EUR** |

When the value is exactly half a cent, it rounds up: one minute at **0.30 EUR/hour** is worth 0.5 cents and becomes **0.01 EUR**. Person/project aggregation does not repeat rounding on the raw sum of minutes.

This rule helps reproduce the result, not artificially choose how many rows to enter. Entries must represent work performed according to adopted conventions.

## 8. What changes when data is modified

Perform these variations only on a lab copy of the dataset. They are independent scenarios: restore the base before each one.

### Changing a past rate

Change Elena's rate valid on 12 October from 60 to **72 EUR/hour**. That day's three hours increase by 36 EUR; 13 October retains 66 EUR/hour. Elena's cost goes from 312 to **348 EUR**, and total known cost from 447 to **483 EUR**. Minutes remain 540, coverage remains 480 minutes and time-entry rows are not rewritten.

This demonstrates that the current estimate is not a frozen actual financial result. The future financial process will need to save separate approved snapshots.

### New contract linked to B

Create a fourth contract ANA-C25 with value **2,500 EUR** and link B to it, retaining the hours. The period's current view will show:

| Contract | Linked hours | Known value |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| ANA-C10, now only A | 4 h 00 min | 10,000.00 EUR |
| ANA-C25, B | 2 h 30 min | 2,500.00 EUR |
| ANA-CND, C | 1 h 00 min | Undefined |
| ANA-C00, E | 0 h 30 min | 0.00 EUR |

Known value becomes **12,500 EUR**, with four distinct contracts and one missing value. D remains without a contract for 60 minutes. The report uses the current link: it does not preserve B's previous link for 12–13 October hours. Labor cost remains 447 EUR with the original rates.

### No hours in the period

Select a date without entries, such as 14 October in the freshly prepared lab. Views must not invent rows for users, projects or contracts without activity. Work counts are zero. A still-populated filter list does not mean hours exist in the period: it helps select other data.

### Only the person without a rate

Filter Davide in Labor cost. The report contains 60 minutes and two entries, all without a rate, with estimate **unavailable**. The known total's internal numeric value may be zero, but the interface interprets it together with coverage: do not read it as an actual zero cost.

## 9. Consistency checks

| Check | Base result |
| --- | --- |
| Sum of the ten rows' minutes | 540 |
| Sum by day, project and person | Always 540 |
| Row count | 10 |
| Priced + unpriced minutes | 480 + 60 = 540 |
| Known cost by day | 225 + 222 = 447 EUR |
| Known cost by person | 312 + 135 + 0 = 447 EUR, Davide excluded because unknown |
| Minutes with + without a contract | 390 + 60 + 30 + 60 = 540 |
| Known value of distinct contracts | 10,000 + 0 = 10,000 EUR; ANA-CND remains unknown |

These behaviors are covered by cases in `tests/test_reports.py` and `tests/test_labor_rates.py`, and queries in `src/workspace.c`. General rules are in [Processes and rules](processi-e-regole.md); for a structured test session, use [acceptance testing](collaudo-accettazione.md).
